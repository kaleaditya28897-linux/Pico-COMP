The intention of this project is to build a modern analog of a TRS-80 model 100. Powered by a couple of microcontrollers and 18650 batteries. 
The device will be text only and use a simple lcd panel with simple monochrome colour modes like Amber on black, Green on black or black on white.
The device will be capable of a full office suite including text documents, spreadsheets and database applications also capable of email and a few basic integrated IM chats over wifi.
The device will be capable of connecting to printers and print documents if required.
The device will be capable of syncing files over wifi by presenting a webpage on the network.
The device will play some basic text based games and have simple beeps for sound during operation.
The device will have a simple text editor where user can write whatever they want or even write code to be worked on further on other machines
The device will have a battery life of 1 week standby and 4 days of use on 4 1800mah 18650 batteries.
The device will charge over usb c 
The device will have a 10 inch screen, a small mechanical keyboard with the arrow keys the only form of navigation with hotkeys assigned for other functions which will be duly written on a sticker on by the base of the screen.
The device will be built on RP2350 microcontrollers and programmed exclusively in C.
The device will also include a scheduler and a todo app.
Additional planned features:
- Productivity: terminal/SSH client, calculator with a programmable BASIC-style interpreter, address book/phone list, notes with search plus outliner/journal, time tracker.
- Connectivity: RSS/news reader, Gopher/Gemini browser, IRC/Matrix client, serial/USB file transfer (XMODEM, USB mass-storage mode).
- Hardware: SD card storage, battery-backed RTC, instant resume, battery gauge with low-battery auto-save, expansion port (GPS, sensors, barcode scanner) with a data logger app.
- Games/utilities: roguelike, text adventure, Tetris/Snake, typing tutor, Pomodoro timer, world clock.
