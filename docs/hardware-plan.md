# Hardware plan

Everything under `hardware/` is generated by scripts. Edit the scripts, regenerate, and open the
results in KiCad / FreeCAD to inspect them. See "Regenerating" at the end.

## Boards (KiCad 10 projects in `hardware/kicad/`)

Each board is a full KiCad project: schematic (`*.kicad_sch`), PCB (`*.kicad_pcb`) and project
libraries (`pico-comp.pretty`, `pico-comp.kicad_sym`, `pico-comp.3dshapes`). The schematic is
generated from the same netlist as the PCB: pins connect through net labels at the pin tips, and the
footprints are linked to their symbols, so ERC, schematic-parity DRC and "Update PCB from Schematic"
all work. Gate for fab output: ERC 0 errors, DRC 0 violations and 0 unconnected (`fab.sh` enforces it).

### Main board (152 x 84 mm, 6 layers: F.Cu sig / In1 GND plane / In2 sig / In3 sig / In4 3V3 plane / B.Cu sig + GND pour)
The board grew from 68 to 84 mm (toward the hinge; the front edge with the RM2 stays put) because the
original placement could not be routed with intact planes. `gen_pcb.py` keeps placing parts in the
68 mm design frame and stretches y by `SY`; edge-referenced parts use `fixed=True`.
Design rules: 0.127 mm (5 mil) trace/space, 0.45/0.2 mm vias; power nets 0.25 mm traces plus pours.
Six layers were needed: on four, the two QFN-60s could only be autorouted by running signals through the
planes. The planes are `LT_POWER` layers so nothing is ever routed on them.
- 2x RP2350A (UI, NET), each with W25Q128JV flash, 12 MHz ABM8-272 crystal, AOTA-B201610S 3.3 uH
  core-regulator inductor (the RP2350 datasheet part), BOOTSEL + RESET buttons, 1.27 mm SWD header.
- Power: USB-C (5.1k CC pull-downs, USBLC6 ESD) -> BQ25798 buck-boost charger -> VSYS -> TPS62933
  5 V / 3 A -> AP63203 3.3 V / 2 A. Pack on XT30PW-M, NTC on JST-PH.
  BQ25798 strapping (datasheet SLUSDV2): PROG 8.2k = 2S, 750 kHz (needs the 2.2 uH inductor);
  ACDRV1/2 to GND (no input FETs); SDRV 1 nF to GND (no ship FET); BATP via 100R; QON open;
  ILIM_HIZ 47k/47k from REGN = ~1.9 A input limit; TS 5.23k/30.1k for a 103AT NTC. TPS62933 RT
  open = 500 kHz.
- Power pours on F.Cu for VBUS, PMID, VSYS, VBAT and 5 V around the converters (the BQ25798's
  0.2 mm pads only take 0.25 mm traces); switch nodes stay short traces. The pours are hidden from the
  autorouter and filled afterwards.
- UI: HSTX -> 270R -> HDMI-A (internal link to the panel, +5 V on pin 18), microSD (SPI0), printer
  JST-SH on UART1, expansion 1x10 1.27 mm (GPIO0-3, 8-11: I2C0, SPI1), piezo beeper on PWM, LED.
- NET: keyboard matrix header (1x20, right angle, pins out of the front edge), BQ25798 on I2C0,
  Raspberry Pi RM2 Wi-Fi (CYW43439, onboard antenna, gSPI wired like the Pico 2 W), UART to UI.
- GPIO map: `firmware/common/pins.h`. CYW43 clock is on GPIO26 (Pico 2 W uses 29): override
  `CYW43_DEFAULT_PIN_WL_CLOCK` in the NET firmware.
- The NET MCU's USB is not brought out: program it over SWD (J4).

### Firmware updates (both MCUs, nothing to open)
- **Normal update:** USB-C to a PC. An "update firmware" hotkey/menu (or `picotool reboot -u`) calls
  `reset_usb_boot()` on the UI MCU, which shows up as a USB drive (RP2350 bootloader); copy one
  `pico-comp.uf2`.
- **NET MCU:** the UI image carries the NET firmware. At boot the UI asks the NET for its version over the
  UART link and, if it differs, rewrites the NET flash over SWD (UI GPIO10 -> NET SWCLK, GPIO11 -> NET SWDIO;
  J4 stays in parallel for a debug probe). New boards: flash the UI once over USB and the NET follows.
- **Recovery:** pinhole in the right wall over the side-push RESET (SW2, Panasonic EVQ-PUJ): double-tap
  = USB update drive (`pico_bootsel_via_double_reset`, linked into the UI firmware). If even that fails:
  BOOTSEL buttons inside (SW1 UI, SW3 NET).
- **NET MCU USB:** its D+/D- (27R) go to internal JST-SH 4-pin J14 (pin 1 VBUS not connected, 2 D-, 3 D+,
  4 GND) for factory or recovery flashing of the NET MCU directly.
- Expansion header J13 now carries 6 GPIOs (0-3, 8, 9) plus GND/3V3 on pins 9/10.

### Keyboard (244 x 120 mm, 2 layers)
- 75 Kailh Choc V1 (CPG135001D01, soldered), 6 rows x 14 columns, 17 mm pitch, inverted-T arrows.
  Stack-up from the Kailh drawing: plate 1.2 mm, its underside 2.2 mm above the PCB; stem top 8.0 mm
  above the PCB; 3.0 mm travel.
- Stabilizers: Kailh PG1350 plate-mount (Kailh drawings BSPG1350-WD002 stem, -WD003 housing,
  BWPG1350-07003 2u wire 24.00 mm, BWPG1350-07002 wire 76.00 mm). 2u kit on Shift, 6.25u kit (76 mm wire)
  on the 6u Space. 6.30 x 6.60 mm plate openings, 7.8 x 8.0 mm PCB clearance cut-outs (footprints
  `Kailh_Choc_Stabilizer_2u/6u`, in the BOM as ST1/ST2), male cross pegs on those keycaps.
- 1N4148W diode per key on the **bottom** side (the switch housing sits flat on the top).
  COL -> switch -> diode anode, cathode -> ROW: drive rows low, read columns with pull-ups.
- 1x20 right-angle header (pins out of the front edge, pin 1 at the right end), 3 M2 holes in the
  front strip, rear edge resting on the case rail, RF keep-out above the mainboard's RM2 antenna.
- `layout.py` is the single source for key positions: `plate.dxf` (laser-cut plate, R4 corners),
  `matrix.csv`, the PCB and the case all come from it.

### Wi-Fi / RF
- RM2 sits at the main board's front edge, antenna end flush with the edge; its 35.5 x 18.5 mm
  keep-out (datasheet Figure 6) has no copper on any layer, and the keyboard PCB has a matching
  keep-out above it. The antenna is under the plastic palm rest, in front of the key plate.
- No extra RF shielding is needed: RM2 has its own can. Keep metal (and a metal plate) away from
  the antenna end. RM2's modular approval does not cover India; a product sold there needs its own
  WPC/ETA approval.

## Mechanical (`hardware/mechanical/`, FreeCAD 1.1)
`case.py` builds the whole assembly with every part as its own object, grouped in the tree:
Case (base, lid, palm rest, key plate, hinge pin), Display, Battery (4 cells, BMS), Mainboard and
Keyboard (bare PCB + one object per component from the KiCad 3D models), Keycaps (75 caps + legend
inlays), Fasteners, Cables. Board positions come from `hardware/placement.py`; port cut-outs and
standoffs come from the board data, and the script fails on real interferences.

Fabrication files:
- `print/base`, `print/lid`, `print/palm_rest`, `print/key_plate` (STEP + STL). The base and lid are
  250 x 140 mm: they need a printer bed of at least 250 mm, or a print service.
- `keyboard/plate.dxf`: laser-cut key plate, 1.2 mm (FR4 or aluminium; the antenna is not under it),
  with the stabilizer openings.
- `keycaps/`: one STEP + STL per keycap, legend debossed 0.5 mm, plus `*_legend` inlay bodies for
  two-colour printing. Choc V1 stem: two 1.1 x 2.9 mm legs, 5.7 mm apart (Kailh drawing: 1.20 x 3.00
  slots). Shift and Space have stabilizer cross pegs: test-print one Shift cap for fit first.

## Power (custom 2S2P pack)
- Pack: 4x 18650 as 2S2P (7.4 V nominal, 6.0-8.4 V), four cells side by side in one layer.
  Spec to the pack maker: balancing BMS with over/under-voltage, over-current, short-circuit and
  NTC protection, matched branded cells, XT30 lead, optional JST-PH 10k NTC lead.

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Configuration | 2S2P, 4x 18650 Li-ion (NMC), branded matched cells |
| Capacity | 6000 mAh (2x 3000 mAh cells) = 44.4 Wh |
| Nominal voltage | 7.4 V (full 8.4 V, cut-off 6.0 V) |
| Continuous / peak discharge | 2 A / 4 A (BMS trip ~6 A) |
| Charge current | set by the BQ25798 (1 A default for 2S, firmware can raise it) |
| Typical load | ~0.6 A at 7.4 V (display 3.1 W + MCUs/Wi-Fi/SD ~1 W) |
| Power connector | XT30 (pack female, board XT30PW-M) |
| NTC | JST-PH 2-pin, 10k 103AT |
| Leads | 100 mm, 18 AWG silicone |

- Battery life: ~4 W in use gives ~10 h continuous; the week-standby target relies on the display
  off and instant resume.

## Manufacturing (robu.in)
robu.in confirmed by phone: PCB fab, turnkey SMT assembly with parts they source, 3D printing,
CNC/laser cutting and custom Li-ion packs.
- PCBs: main board is 6 layers, 1.6 mm, 5 mil / 0.2 mm drill. Send `hardware/fab/<board>-gerbers.zip`, `hardware/fab/<board>/assembly/*-bom.csv` (MPN and
  spec columns) and `*-cpl.csv` (pick and place). Main board: SMT top only. Keyboard: diodes on the
  bottom, switches hand-soldered.
- Plate: `plate.dxf`. Case and keycaps: `print/` and `keycaps/`. Battery: the order sheet above.
- Ask them: 6-layer capability at 0.127 / 0.127 mm with 0.45 / 0.2 mm vias, stack-up, stock of
  RP2350A, BQ25798 and RM2.

## Open items (need a decision or a measurement)
- **Measure**: panel module thickness and outline, the HDMI cable/plug (a slim or FPC HDMI cable is
  assumed). The keycap stabilizer peg dimensions come from the stem drawing: test-fit one cap.
- **Datasheet review** before ordering: passive values around the BQ25798, TPS62933 and AP63203
  (the topology follows the datasheets; values are typical-application values).

## Regenerating
```
python3 hardware/keyboard/layout.py                # plate.dxf, matrix.csv
python3 hardware/kicad/make_footprints.py          # RM2 + Choc footprints
FreeCADCmd hardware/kicad/make_3d.py               # STEP models KiCad's library lacks
python3 hardware/mainboard/gen_pcb.py && python3 hardware/mainboard/gen_sch.py
python3 hardware/keyboard/gen_pcb.py  && python3 hardware/keyboard/gen_sch.py   # after mainboard (RF keep-out)
# main board: routed by hand in KiCad (docs/mainboard-routing.md); gen_pcb.py won't overwrite tracks
python3 hardware/mainboard/route.py hardware/kicad/keyboard.kicad_pcb 15
sh hardware/kicad/fab.sh                           # ERC/DRC gate + all fab outputs -> hardware/fab/
python3 hardware/kicad/export_parts.py             # part placement + copper/silk layer STEPs for FreeCAD
FreeCADCmd hardware/mechanical/case.py             # assembly, print files, keycaps
python3 hardware/mechanical/build_viewer.py        # offline HTML viewer
```
